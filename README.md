# About Me

Hi, I'm **Ruva** — a third-year Artificial Intelligence student who likes turning ideas into things that actually work.

I'm especially interested in **Deep Learning, Computer Vision, NLP, Robotics, Quantum Computing, and the human mind**.

Most of my time is spent somewhere between training models, building applications, reading papers I probably shouldn't have opened at 2 AM, and trying to figure out why the model decided that a completely normal image is a banana.

### Currently

* Deep Learning & Computer Vision
* Natural Language Processing
* Building practical AI projects
* Exploring Robotics
* Learning more about Quantum Machine Learning
